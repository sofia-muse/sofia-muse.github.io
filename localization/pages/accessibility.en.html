Accessibility statement

This statement describes the site's accessibility features and remaining checks. It is a draft, not a certification of compliance.

Features available

  • Keyboard navigation, visible focus, a skip link and structured headings.
  • Form labels and dialogs designed to manage focus and close with Escape.
  • Text size options up to 150%, increased spacing, contrast and link emphasis.
  • Controls to pause animation and hide the drone, plus reduced-motion support.
  • English, Ukrainian, Russian and Hebrew, including right-to-left layouts.

Open the accessibility button to adjust the display. Settings are saved between visits only when you allow preference storage.

Checks still needed

Keyboard and responsive checks have been carried out during development. A complete audit with NVDA or VoiceOver has not been completed. The CV PDF and all interactive project demonstrations have not undergone a full accessibility audit.

No claim of complete WCAG compliance, external certification or legal exemption is made. Further testing and review may identify additional barriers.

Report a difficulty

Contact Sofia Museichuk at sofia.museichuk@gmail.com if you cannot use part of the site or need information in another format.

If possible, include the page address, the action you tried, your browser and any assistive technology. Share only what is needed to explain the problem. Sofia will review the request and discuss a suitable way to help.

Before publication

The owner must confirm the statement's accuracy, complete the remaining checks and obtain advice on which Israeli accessibility obligations apply.
