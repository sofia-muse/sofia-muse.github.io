Privacy policy

Who is responsible?

Sofia Museichuk manages this portfolio and enquiries. Contact: sofia.museichuk@gmail.com.

Your enquiry

The form prepares a draft in your browser. It does not send your message automatically. If you send it through your email app, Sofia receives your sender details and message to answer questions, discuss a project and manage the resulting correspondence.

Providing information is voluntary. Please include a way to reply and enough detail to discuss your request. Without these, a useful response may not be possible. Do not include passwords, access tokens or private customer data.

Cookies and saved settings

There are no analytics or advertising trackers. The necessary cookie sofia_portfolio_consent stores your choice for 180 days. Only with your permission, local storage saves accessibility and motion preferences under sofia-accessibility and sofia-motion-paused. Withdrawing permission removes these saved preferences.

Hosting and email providers

The site is intended for GitHub Pages hosting. A hosting provider may process technical requests, including IP addresses and request logs. Email providers process sender details and messages when you send an enquiry. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

Access and correction

Contact Sofia to request access to or correction of your personal information, subject to applicable law and identity verification where needed.
